## 0. One reduction, recorded first

The previous round left "a `U(1)` direction and a sign" unselected. The
`U(1)` direction is the choice of a unit imaginary quaternion `u` in the
plane orthogonal to the fibre generator; conjugation by `Spin(2)` (a fibre
rotation) carries any such `u` to any other. So the geometry selects the
**conjugacy class** `{u ∈ Pin⁻(2) ∖ Spin(2) : u² = −1}`, which is all an
invariant statement can select. The direction is gauge and is removed from
the "unselected physical data" column before this round begins. What
remains is the **sign**: the Pin⁻ structure.

Nothing here uses a singlet, a Born rule, a projector, a tensor product,
CHSH, a QED amplitude or a quantum path integral.

## 1. Targets, with falsifiers

Model the round mouth as `S² = {x ∈ Im H : |x| = 1}` and the bulk mouth as
`S³ = {q ∈ H : |q| = 1}`, with the brane normal at the mouth centre
identified with the real axis `1 ∈ H` (geometric: the pole of the mouth
`S³` in the brane-normal direction) and a reference brane direction `i`
(gauge: any other is conjugate by `SO(3)`).

* **T1 (the spin-frame map).** `q ↦ (x, e₁, e₂) = (q⁻¹iq, q⁻¹jq, q⁻¹kq)`
  sends `S³` onto the oriented orthonormal frame bundle `F_SO(S²) ≅ SO(3)`
  as the double cover `Spin(3) → SO(3)` with kernel `{±1}`: `x ∈ S²`,
  `(e₁, e₂)` orthonormal and tangent at `x`, `det(e₁, e₂, x) = +1`, and
  `q, −q` have the same image. *Falsifier:* any of these fails, or a pair
  `q ≠ ±q′` with the same frame.
* **T2 (fibre angle `φ` → frame angle `2φ`).** Under the repository's fibre
  action `q ↦ e^{iφ}q`, `x` is fixed and `(e₁, e₂)` rotates by `2φ`
  (`e₁ ↦ cos 2φ e₁ − sin 2φ e₂` up to the orientation convention). The
  pulled-back Levi-Civita connection of the round `S²`,
  `ω = ⟨ė₁, e₂⟩`, equals twice the Hopf connection `A = ⟨iq, q̇⟩` along
  every path (checked on random paths to `1e-10`). Consequently
  `c₁(Hopf) = 1` (the repository's own `hopf.chern`) against
  `e(TS²) = χ(S²) = 2` (Gauss–Bonnet, `∫K dA / 2π`): the factor two **is**
  `Spin(2) → SO(2)`. *Falsifier:* `ω ≠ 2A`, or the two numbers are not in
  ratio `1 : 2`.
* **T3 (the deck lift).** The antipode `A: x ↦ −x` reverses the orientation
  of `S²`, so its natural lift `dA` sends oriented frames to anti-oriented
  ones. Every lift of `A` to the oriented spin-frame bundle is
  `L_u : q ↦ uq` with `u ⊥ i`, `|u| = 1`: `x(uq) = −x(q)`, the frame image
  is `dA` composed with one reflection (orientation restored), and
  `L_u² = L_{−1}`, the central element — the deck transformation of
  `Spin(3) → SO(3)`, i.e. the `2π` rotation. The set of such lifts is one
  `Spin(2)`-conjugacy class (a circle), the fibre-reversing component. On
  the two-sheeted Pin⁻(2) bundle of `S²` the involution is
  `Ã = (L_u on +, L_{−u} on −)` with `Ã² = 1`; the two choices of sign are
  the two Pin⁻ structures of `RP²`. The BAM map `σ = L_{−j}` is the member
  `u = −j`, and the previous round's intrinsic holonomy is the same class.
  *Falsifier:* `L_u` fails to cover `A` for some `u ⊥ i`, or covers it for
  some `u` not `⊥ i`, or `L_u² ≠ −1`.
* **T4 (the two Pin⁻ structures, without preference).** Enumerated as
  `ε = ±1` (the sign of `u` on the `+` sheet). Neither is preferred by the
  metric or by `ι`.
* **T5 (ABK invariants).** For `H₁(RP²; Z₂) = Z₂ = {0, g}` the Pin⁻
  structure `ε` gives the `Z₄`-quadratic enhancement `q(g) = ε mod 4`
  (`q(0) = 0`), which is quadratic: `q(g+g) = 2q(g) + 2(g·g) mod 4` with
  `g·g = 1`. The Gauss sum `Σ_x i^{q(x)} = 1 + i^{ε} = √2 e^{iεπ/4}` gives
  `ABK = ε mod 8`: the two structures carry `+1` and `−1 = 7`. The `H¹`
  action shifts `q(g)` by `2`, exchanging them. *Falsifier:* the Gauss sum
  has modulus `≠ √2` or the two invariants are equal.
* **T6 (pair nucleation as Pin⁻ bordism).** With `Ω₂^{Pin⁻} ≅ Z₈`
  generated by `RP²` (imported: Kirby–Taylor / Anderson–Brown–Peterson),
  a Pin⁻ 3-manifold `W` with `∂W = RP²_a ⊔ RP²_b` exists iff
  `ABK_a + ABK_b ≡ 0 mod 8`. With `ABK = ±1`: **opposite** sectors bound
  (`1 + 7 = 8`), equal sectors do not (`2` or `14`), and a single `RP²`
  does not (`±1`). *Falsifier:* equal sectors sum to `0 mod 8`.
  *Modelling assumption, chosen:* that pair creation is a Pin⁻ bordism of
  the mouth surfaces, i.e. that the mouths' Pin⁻ structures are induced
  from a Pin⁻ structure on the worldvolume.
* **T7 (exclusions).** No Bell state, Born rule, QED or path integral.
