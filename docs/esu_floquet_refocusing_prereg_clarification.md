# Dated clarification to the ESU Floquet freeze (2026-09-26)

This note clarifies, and does not edit,
[the freeze `4e65c3e`](esu_floquet_refocusing_prereg.md). No equation,
observable, threshold or verdict rule changes.

Section 1 of the freeze opens: "#308–#309 closed the throat as a classical
channel". That overstates what those PRs established.

- **What they established.** On the saved final slice of a short
  (t ≤ .01), spherically symmetric evolution, the neck spheres are future
  trapped in both metric frames. The Einstein-frame scalar system satisfies
  the null energy condition wherever f > 0.
- **What they did not establish.**
  - an event horizon;
  - two isolated black holes;
  - the neck's long-time behaviour;
  - a global exclusion of every causal route through the neck.
- **What #309 requires.** Before any later response is attributed to the
  exterior, #309's design demands a causal-path (timing) check.

The pinch timescale of about .03 was measured in a separate reviewer
evolution, not in the #308 archive. The general arguments cited alongside it
(Fuller–Wheeler, Hochberg–Visser flare-out) are motivations, not results
proved here.

The Floquet question does not depend on this premise. Antipodal propagation
through the exterior bulk is a valid research target whether or not some
route through a neck survives. The refocusing and stability results of
`esu_floquet_refocusing.md` concern the handle-free ESU and are unaffected.
