Equations, review correction, and limits

Fixed neck: R₀(x) = b cosh[acosh(1/b) x/h], h = 0.6. The original signed wave gives R = R₀ + u. With G(s) = exp(−s²/ε²), the displayed field is ψ = G(ρ−R) + G(ρ+R) − G(ρ−R)G(ρ+R). Its even analytic dependence on ρ makes it smooth in three-dimensional Cartesian coordinates, including the axis, for finite ε. Negative R is allowed as a signed field parameter; ψ is unchanged under R → −R. It is then no longer an embedded mid-surface radius.

The previous version kept the mid-surface radius at ε at contact, but the inner offset R−ε still closed at one axis point. Its cap jump was hidden by a dissolve. Both the imposed caps and that dissolve are removed here. This selects review option C. The mathematical field is smooth; we have not established a nonsingular spacetime metric.

The old radial-band criterion has threshold A ≥ b−ε. Equality just grazes contact at t = 4.25; a strict inequality gives a closure interval. The ψ = ½ contour uses the different radius threshold ε sqrt[−log(1−sqrt(1/2))]. At default parameters, both criteria close and reopen as the packet passes. Changing ε changes the resolution-dependent topology. No minimum-radius clamp, contact event handler, or alternate geometry enters ψ.

The full-neck plot uses different axial/radial scales for readability; the focus view has equal scales.
