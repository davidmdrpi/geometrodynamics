Geometry and scope

The initial neck is the equilibrium catenoid at rim radius a = 1 and half-separation h = 0.6. Its initial waist is b ≈ 0.745071. The fixed-rim cosh family R = b cosh[acosh(1/b) z/h] blends into R = sqrt[b² + (1−b²)(z/h)²] as the waist narrows. Only the initial member is asserted to be an equilibrium catenoid.

The latter profile approaches a double cone as b tends to zero, but this study stops at a finite cutoff. Two closed caps are inserted by a stated geometric replacement and then flatten. This is a visualization of the proposed topology rule, not a computation of self-contact, fluid dynamics, GR, or quantization. A coupled membrane calculation is needed to determine whether an incoming wave actually produces the depicted evolution.
