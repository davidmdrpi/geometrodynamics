## 0. Two retractions from the previous round, recorded first

**R1 — "the Pin types do not match" is withdrawn.** The previous round
found the bulk quotient `M/ι = RP⁴ # RP⁴` to be Pin⁺ only and the brane neck
`RP²` to be Pin⁻ only, and called that a mismatch. A submanifold need not
carry the ambient Pin type; the normal bundle participates. With
`a ∈ H¹(RP²; Z₂)` the generator and the two normal directions to the neck
(the throat direction `∂_s` and the brane normal `n`) both reversed by the
deck generator,

```
ν = λ ⊕ λ,      w(ν) = (1+a)² = 1 + a²
w(TM|_N) = w(TN) w(ν) = (1 + a + a²)(1 + a²) = 1 + a        (a³ = 0)
```

so `w₂(TM|_N) = 0` (consistent with ambient Pin⁺) while intrinsically
`w₂(TN) + w₁(TN)² = a² + a² = 0` (Pin⁻). Ambient Pin⁺ plus two twisted
normal lines **induces** intrinsic Pin⁻. That is the mechanism, not an
incompatibility. Verified by hand before freezing.

**R2 — "`J` is not a lift of any gluing map" is withdrawn.** The real map
under `J` is `σ = L_{−j} ∈ SO(4)`, which has the Spin(4) lift `(−j, 1)` (up
to the central sign), acting on one half-spinor as the linear quaternion
`−j`. What is true is narrower: `J` is not a lift of `ι` or of `−I₄`, and the
geometry does not select `m = σ` as the seam monodromy. The `K` arises
because `L_{−j} L_i = −L_i L_{−j}`: antilinearity with respect to one
complex structure, linearity with respect to another, for one real map.

## 1. The question

Restrict the ambient Pin⁺ structure of the quotient to the brane neck
`N = RP²` through its normal bundle, compute the holonomy of the deck
generator on the restricted spinor bundle, and compare the result with
`L_{−j}`, `iσ_y`, `iσ_y K`. Three outcomes, fixed in advance:

* **A** — the induced mouth holonomy is canonically equivalent to the BAM
  transport;
* **B** — it satisfies `H̃² = −1` but leaves an unfixed rotation and/or sign;
* **C** — it is inequivalent to `J`.

Nothing here uses a singlet, a Born rule, a projector, a tensor product, CHSH
or a QED amplitude. No Pauli matrix is a starting point: the Clifford
algebras are built as their own regular representations, the quaternions from
their multiplication table.

## 2. Predictions, with falsifiers

Frame at a neck point `Ω ∈ S² ⊂ S³_{s=0}`: `(∂_s, n, t₁, t₂)` with `n` the
brane normal inside the mouth `S³` and `t₁, t₂` tangent to the neck `S²`.
The deck generator of `RP²` lifts to the great semicircle from `Ω` to `−Ω`
in the `t₁` direction, followed by the deck map `ι`.

* **P1 (normal bundle).** Parallel transport along the semicircle, computed
  by integrating `v′ = −(v·γ′)γ` on the round neck, followed by `dι`, gives
  the holonomy `H = diag(−1, −1, +1, −1)` in `(∂_s, n, t₁, t₂)`: both
  normals reversed, the tangent block a reflection. `det H = −1`.
  *Falsifier:* either normal returns unreversed.
* **P2 (Stiefel–Whitney).** In `Z₂[a]/(a³)`: `w(ν) = 1 + a²`,
  `w(TN) = 1 + a + a²`, `w(TM|_N) = 1 + a`; `w₂(TM|_N) = 0`;
  `w₂(TN) + w₁(TN)² = 0`; `w₂(TN) = a² ≠ 0`. So the ambient is Pin⁺-
  compatible on `N` and the intrinsic structure is Pin⁻ and not Pin⁺.
* **P3 (the conversion).** With `e_s, e_n` the normal Clifford generators
  (`e² = +1`, Pin⁺), the twisted tangent generators `ẽ_t = e_t e_s e_n`
  satisfy `ẽ_t² = −1` and `ẽ_{t₁}ẽ_{t₂} = −ẽ_{t₂}ẽ_{t₁}`: they generate
  `Cl⁻(2) ≅ H`. The normal volume element has `(e_s e_n)² = −1`.
  *Falsifier:* `ẽ_t² = +1`.
* **P4 (the holonomy lift).** The Pin⁺ lift of `H` is `±e_s e_n e_{t₂} = ±ẽ_{t₂}`,
  odd in `Cl(4)`, chirality-exchanging, with `H̃² = −1`. In Pin⁻ the same
  product squares to `+1`. *Falsifier:* `H̃² = +1` in Pin⁺.
* **P5 (consistency with the cover).** `H̃²` is the spin holonomy of the
  closed great circle `γ ∪ ι(γ)` on the double cover. That holonomy,
  computed by deforming latitude circles continuously to the equator and
  unwrapping the transported angle `2π(1 − cos θ₀) → 2π`, is `−1`. So the
  Pin⁺ sign convention is the one coherent with the cover's spin structure,
  and Pin⁻ (`H̃² = +1`) is not. *Falsifier:* the unwrapped angle is not `2π`.
* **P6 (comparison with `J`).** At the vector level `H` (`det −1`,
  eigenvalues `−1,−1,−1,+1`) and `σ = L_{−j}` (`det +1`, isoclinic) are in
  different `O(4)` classes: not equivalent. At the ambient spinor level
  `H̃` is odd and `σ̃ = (−j,1)` is even: not conjugate in Pin(4). At the
  **intrinsic** level: `Cl⁻(2) ≅ H` with `ẽ_{t₁} ↦ i`, `ẽ_{t₂} ↦ j`,
  `ẽ_{t₁}ẽ_{t₂} ↦ k`; Pin⁻(2) is the group generated by the unit vectors
  `cos α i + sin α j` and `Spin(2) = {e^{θk}}`, i.e. the normaliser
  `Pin(2) ⊂ SU(2)` that the previous round found as the Hopf-fibre-
  preserving isometry group. The mouth holonomy `ẽ_{t₂}` is left
  multiplication by a unit quaternion in the `(i, j)` plane, of order four,
  determined up to conjugation by `Spin(2)` (the tangent direction of the
  path) and up to sign. `σ = L_{−j}` is one such element. Expected outcome:
  **B**, with the unfixed data being a `U(1)` (not `SU(2)`) direction and a
  sign. *Falsifier for A:* a canonical choice of `t₂` and sign from the
  geometry. *Falsifier for B:* `ẽ_{t₂}` not conjugate to `L_{−j}` under
  `Spin(2)` and sign.
* **P7 (the `K`).** In the module `H`, `ẽ_{t₂}` anticommutes with the
  `Spin(2)` generator `k = ẽ_{t₁}ẽ_{t₂}` acting on the left and commutes with
  right multiplication. So it is antilinear for the complex structure `L_k`
  and linear for `R_i`. If the Hopf fibre phase of the mouth is identified
  with the `Spin(2)` rotation of the mouth's tangent plane, the transport is
  antilinear canonically — the `K` is the statement that Pin⁻(2) ∖ Spin(2)
  anticommutes with the Spin(2) generator. That identification is physical
  and unproved: **conditional**.
* **P8 (selection).** `RP²` has two Pin⁻ structures (`H¹ = Z₂`), the
  quotient four Pin⁺ structures, the cover `S³ × S¹` two spin structures.
  The sign of `H̃` is not fixed by the metric or by `ι`; it is the choice
  of Pin structure. Expected: **not selected** by the geometry. This is the
  quantity `embedding/topology.py` stores as `wrap_parity`. *Falsifier:* a
  geometric invariant that distinguishes `+H̃` from `−H̃`.

## 3. Verdict rule

* **A** requires P1–P7 and a canonical `t₂` and sign (P8 selected).
* **B** requires P1–P7 with P8 unselected.
* **C** if P4 fails (`H̃² ≠ −1` in the Pin type consistent with P5) or P6's
  intrinsic conjugacy fails.
