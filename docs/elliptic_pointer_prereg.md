# Frozen experiment: one classical record from an elliptic mouth

Parent: merged PR #356 at `85c5f68`. The quadrupole matrix element is from [PR #241](https://github.com/davidmdrpi/geometrodynamics/pull/241). The two real quadratures of each classical winding mode are canonical variables. Use the normalized complex amplitudes as coordinates for them. Freeze this protocol before running the probe.

* In the winding basis use the Hermitian matrix with zero diagonal and upper-right entry exp(iφ). Call its classical quadratic form z. Keep the relative phase of the field. Equal intensities in its two eigenchannels mean z=0. Equal winding intensities can also give z=±1 as the relative phase changes.
* During writing use unit mass, unit spring constant, damping 0.4, and interaction energy −g q z with g=0.1(2−√2). This uses the magnitude of the eight-site coefficient and chooses the displacement scale and its sign. Exchange the sign of z as a control. Implement the reciprocal field equation. Model damping by a string initially free of incident waves.
* Prepare the pointer at rest. End the write at the first half-period of the damped oscillator. Disconnect the field and switch the pointer potential to (q²−1)²/4. Account for the switch work and outgoing energy. Follow the free pointer for 80 time units. Count a latched record only if the energy is below the central barrier, the distance to one minimum is below 0.001 and the speed is below 0.001. Keep every unresolved run in the denominator. A centered pointer at z=0 is an explicit no-record control.
* Primary preparation: q is uniform on [−0.12,0.12], independent of the field. Use 256 midpoint quantiles per input and double to 512. Sweep z from −1 to 1 in steps of 0.25, including equal intensities. Compare positive-record frequencies with (1+z)/2 without fitting. A primary discrepancy above 0.02 on any input rejects the proposed probability law for this preparation. Check the exact switch map and basin counts against evolution; flag any count difference above one quantile. Report the largest normalized-field norm error and energy-accounting residual, targeting 1e−8 and 1e−7 respectively.
* Diagnostic controls: solve analytically for a uniform preparation width that would give the target frequencies, then change only the preparation density at that same width to the projection of a uniform circle angle. These controls test whether matching probabilities identifies a geometric mechanism. Also change the write time by ±10% with the primary ensemble at 256 quantiles and report the measured curves.
* Scope: the latch, switch, load and preparation are detector inputs. This is a single-detector test of record formation in a two-mode reduction. Throat support, gravitational backreaction, leakage to other windings and two-detector correlations require separate experiments.
