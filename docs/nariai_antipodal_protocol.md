# Antipodal Nariai-loop gravity probe

Fix five-dimensional Einstein gravity, 8πG=1, Λ=3, S¹×S³ initial topology, unwrapped cover time, and round S³ orbits. Units set the Nariai areal radius to one. Test the free involution J:(t,x)→(−t,x+L/2), L=2π. Equivariance of data includes the whole spatial metric and extrinsic curvature. Do not impose opposite velocities at equal positive times.

Vacuum controls: check the momentum constraint for an undistorted constant-radius vacuum slice. Construct a nonconstant-radius compact near-extremal vacuum development with two copies of a neck and a smooth half-period-odd time tilt localized about them. Use the continued Kottler mass, signed null expansions, curvature and exact central normal proper times to classify the two fates. Include the homogeneous Nariai limit. Separate the slicing-induced velocity packet from propagating radiation.

Propagating control: add a canonical massless scalar and solve its coupled Einstein equations on the circle in conformal gauge. Set r=0.98, σ=0, radius velocity U=−A f³ with f=cos(x) exp[(cos(2x)−1)/4], positive constant scalar normal velocity and scalar gradient determined by the momentum constraint. Choose the smooth longitudinal velocity from the Hamiltonian constraint. The scalar changes sign under J (shift symmetry fixes its additive constant). Primary A=0.05, controls A=0 and 0.025, 0.10. Use at least three spatial resolutions for the primary, track both centers, initial constraints, evolved null constraints, an outgoing null ray, angular sectional curvature, and cutoff sensitivity. Count a bounce only upon resolved contraction-to-expansion at positive radius. A small grid radius alone cannot establish a singularity. Distinguish finite-affine focusing from a computed endpoint; report gauge or global-grid failures explicitly. No switch to a time quotient in the evolution and no inference of stability outside this sector.
