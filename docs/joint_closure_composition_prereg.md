# Round 10 pre-registration: what data does joint closure retain?

**Status: protocol draft for review; no round-10 implementation or numerical
experiment has been run.** Prepared 2026-09-07 against main
`22f77a373a67fcda91078ad0284be6a37c7ca20b`, which includes #286 and #287.
Publication records this proposed protocol before implementation. Its accepted
commit must be named in any later implementation; subsequent changes must be
recorded explicitly rather than silently replacing a failed criterion.

**Inherited conditioning is CHOSEN.** The reference preparation is product
Haar measure and separate windows in each triangle's phase, with both 0 and
pi closure branches retained. Round 8 established that phase conditioning
is justified by the repository's phase axiom but is not forced by the
zero set. Conditioning on the numerators instead gives a different measure.
Nothing in this round may promote that choice to a geometric necessity.

The question is whether existing BAM closure and composition rules require
the joint weight to depend on the two triangle invariants separately, on
their product alone, or on additional joint invariants. A distinction is
essential: the independent reference preparation already has a product
coarea density. That does not establish a universal scalar description of
all joint histories, weights or readouts.

## 1. Prior state: results known before this protocol

These are analytic inputs and regression oracles, not discoveries or open
questions for round 10. The positive-power calculation and cubic
counterexample were independently checked in the conversation preceding
this draft. Their reproduction may check an implementation, but may not be
counted as evidence that BAM selects a composition law.

### P1. Independent product preparation alone selects no counting function

Round 9 uses, with paired sector priors,

\[
 D=t+\sqrt{2t}\cos\psi,\quad
 W_\Phi(t)=\int_0^{2\pi}\Phi(D)\,d\psi,\quad
 E(\gamma)=\frac{W_\Phi(1-\cos\gamma)-W_\Phi(1+\cos\gamma)}
 {W_\Phi(1-\cos\gamma)+W_\Phi(1+\cos\gamma)}.
\]

For any admissible nonnegative `Phi` with finite, nonzero normalization,
the independent extension has density `Phi(D1) Phi(D2)` relative to product
arclength. It is associative and marginalizes to each subsystem. The cubic
`Phi(D)=D^2(1-D/5)` remains nonnegative because its two arguments remain in
`[-1/2,4]`; this construction never evaluates the cubic at `D1 D2`.

### P2. No admissible power law gives the singlet law in round 9's transform

This result is **independent of any composition assumption**. For positive
real `p`, define `Phi(D)=|D|^p`. The established endpoint argument is

\[
 W_p(t)\sim C_p t^{p/2},\qquad
 C_p=2^{p/2}\int_0^{2\pi}|\cos\psi|^p\,d\psi.
\]

The singlet condition is `W(t)/W(2-t)=t/(2-t)`. Its exponent requires
`p=2`, but its coefficient already fails:

\[
 W_2(t)=2\pi t(t+1),\quad
 \frac{C_2}{W_2(2)}=\frac16\ne\frac12.
\]

Thus no positive exponent works. The constant weight `p=0` gives `E=0`.
The exact quadratic correlation and standard-angle CHSH are

\[
 E_2(\gamma)=\frac{-3\cos\gamma}{2+\cos^2\gamma},\qquad
 S_{\rm standard}=\frac{12\sqrt2}{5}=3.394112549695\ldots.
\]

For clarity about the word "admissible," singular negative exponents are
not an escape. For `p<=-1`, generic interior closure integrals diverge at
simple zeros of `D`. For `-1<p<0`, interpreted as improper integrals, the
endpoint ratio tends to infinity rather than zero. Near the other endpoint
`W_p(2-t)` is finite for `p>-1/2`, grows logarithmically for `p=-1/2`, and
scales as `t^(p+1/2)` for `p<-1/2`; combined with `W_p(t)~C_p t^(p/2)`,
each case has the wrong limit. This domain bookkeeping was made explicit
while drafting, not inferred from a future parameter scan. Undefined
infinity-over-infinity probabilities are not candidate laws.

A fitted exponent at one finite `t` is neither a universal exponent nor
attainability. The pre-draft review reported a drifting fitted exponent,
approaching `2` only logarithmically; no fitting exercise is required here.

### P3. Scalar-product universality would obstruct the entire power-law route

This is a **conditional** result. Its hypotheses must be established
separately before it can bear on BAM:

1. Joint histories retain only the scalar `D12=D1 D2` for the relevant weight.
2. The same counting rule applies to single and composite systems and agrees
   with independent product preparation.
3. After the irrelevant scale is removed, the rule is nontrivial,
   nonnegative and continuous, with `f(1)=1`.
4. For the global classification, `f` extends to the multiplicative domain
   generated by arbitrarily many factors from `[-1/2,4]`, which is the real
   line, and obeys `f(xy)=f(x)f(y)` there. This is an additional domain
   hypothesis, not an unnoticed enlargement of the single-triangle range.

These hypotheses give power laws, hence P2 excludes the singlet law.
**Multiplicativity alone leaves the exponent free.** The singlet endpoint
forces the attempted value `p=2`; composition does not independently fix it.

If only in-range pairs are licensed, report that local functional equation
separately. Do not cite the global classification without establishing its
domain hypothesis or an appropriate local theorem.

The cubic's exclusion from a product-only representation needs neither an
extension outside its range nor the classification theorem. For
`f(D)=Phi(D)/Phi(1)`,

\[
 f(1)f(4)=4\ne9=f(2)f(2),\qquad 1\cdot4=2\cdot2.
\]

An overall normalization cannot remove differing relative weights within
the same preparation. This counterexample excludes that scalar compression,
not the independent cubic product construction in P1.

### P4. The quadratic law is an existing cross-round oracle, not a field map

Round 7's local-square enumeration already has
`<D_s^2>=(1+c_s)(2+c_s)` and the same `E_2`, `S_standard`. Its audited five
null stresses and mouth current are degree-2 homogeneous in their specified
scaling variables. That is supporting physical motivation for examining
quadratic responses, but it does not establish `Phi(D)=D^2`: the map from
field amplitudes to `D` and an event-counting coupling are still absent.
The perfect-fluid row scales `u.k`, not a field amplitude, and the audit
covers those six quantities rather than every BAM observable. Keep the
existing N6/N7 qualifications in [history_action.md](history_action.md).

No Gleason theorem, quantization assumption or Hilbert-space measurement
postulate is load-bearing in this protocol. The existing
[tensor-product construction](tensor_product_emergence.md) imports
quantization and cannot certify the classical composition rule sought here.

## 2. Fixed geometric setup and the reference measure

For `i=1,2`, fix noncollinear unit settings `(a_i,b_i)` and sector signs
`s_Ai,s_Bi`. Use round 9's singlet convention consistently:

\[
 u_i=s_{Ai}a_i,\quad w_i=-s_{Bi}b_i,\quad x_i\in S^2,\quad
 q_i=u_i\times w_i,\quad N_i=x_i\cdot q_i,
\]
\[
 D_i=1+u_i\cdot w_i+x_i\cdot(u_i+w_i),\qquad
 \theta_i=\operatorname{atan2}(N_i,D_i).
\]

Let `Gamma_i` be the regular part of `N_i=0`, excluding undefined geodesic
legs at `x_i=-u_i,-w_i`. Retain both signs of nonzero `D_i`. No shared
source direction, common hidden variable, bridge, identification of source
frames or new interaction is imposed between independently prepared copies.
The ambient metric is the product round metric on `S2 x S2`.

The reference prior is product Haar with equal prior mass `1/16` on the
16 joint sectors. Impose **both** conditions
`dist(theta_i, pi Z)<epsilon_i` and then take both widths to zero.
Normalize once over all 16 sectors, not separately inside each sector.
Retain geometric factors `|q_i|` until their permitted cancellations have
been shown. Throughout, `theta=Omega/2`; do not silently mix their window
normalizations.

The following are already-known design consequences, not an open discovery:

\[
 \Gamma_{12}=\Gamma_1\times\Gamma_2,\qquad
 J_\theta=\sqrt{\det(dF\,dF^*)}
 =\frac{|q_1||q_2|}{|D_1D_2|},\quad F=(\theta_1,\theta_2),
\]
\[
 d\mu^\theta_{12}\propto
 \frac{|D_1D_2|}{|q_1||q_2|}\,d\sigma_1d\sigma_2.
\]

The independent factors occupy orthogonal tangent blocks. This explains the
product Jacobian under the stated choices. The reference density therefore
factors through `|D1 D2|` at fixed settings. It does not imply that arbitrary
admissible weights must do so, or that a composite triangle with invariant
`D12` has been constructed. `D1 D2` initially names a product of existing
numbers, not the denominator of a newly derived holonomy.

As a conditioning control, `F_N=(N1,N2)` has the same regular locus and
constant normal Jacobian `|q1||q2|`, hence uniform product arclength. This
is inherited round-8 nonuniqueness, now on the product space.

Do not replace the two closure conditions by the single condition
`theta1+theta2=0 mod pi`. That permits cancellation between nonclosed
subsystems and generically changes a rank-two constraint to rank one.
It is a different physical boundary problem, not a change of coordinates
on the same independent closure locus.

## 3. What "sufficient statistic" will mean here

Fix settings, sectors, the geometric reference measure and the physical
rule under examination before testing a statistic. The primary meaning is
**factorization of the weight density through a prescribed geometric map**:

| Candidate | Required statement on the regular joint locus |
|---|---|
| `S_pair=(D1,D2)` | `rho(x1,x2)=H(D1,D2)` |
| `S_product=D1 D2` | `rho(x1,x2)=h(D1 D2)` |
| Additional joint data | Some physically required density or closure condition cannot factor through `S_pair` |

The first two are nested, not mutually exclusive: a product-only density
is also a function of the pair. Report the smallest demonstrated reduction
and what rule it describes. If it factors through `|D1 D2|`, record that
additional loss of sign information explicitly.

A reduction of **one reference density** is distinct from a statistic
sufficient for a **family of allowed weights or measurements**. For the
latter, every member must factor through the proposed map, with a physical
reason the allowed family is exhaustive. The reference coarea calculation
cannot supply that reason. Do not define the statistic to be the density
itself after seeing the result.

This is not a claim of Fisher–Neyman sufficiency across varying settings.
The supports themselves vary with settings, and may be mutually singular.
Nor does a density factorization reconstruct every source observable:
different points on a level set can remain distinct field histories.
Preserve the induced pushforward measure and the measure within each level
set; integrating over the statistic with flat measure would change the model.

## 4. Questions to be answered after the protocol is accepted

### Q1. Construct and verify the independent joint closure geometry

Compute the closure map, its rank, induced tangent metric, branch structure,
normal Gram matrix and coarea density from the product geometry. Verify
the formulas in section 2 by an independent tangent-coordinate calculation
and by finite phase windows. Report support, unnormalized sector masses and
normalized probabilities separately. Independent subsystem marginals and
three-factor associativity are regression controls, not selection evidence.

Treat punctures explicitly: prove that their excised contribution vanishes
for the reference measure, and compare numerical excisions. Exclude exactly
collinear settings from the regular-circle calculation; a rank-changing
limit is not a valid substitute for the missing case.

### Q2. Audit whether BAM requires a different joint closure or weight rule

Search the pinned repository's classical loop and network machinery before
introducing any joint invariant. Starting points are:

- [history/closure.py](../geometrodynamics/history/closure.py): closure of
  every loop, its event and branch data;
- [bulk/closure_current.py](../geometrodynamics/bulk/closure_current.py) and
  [bulk/history_action.py](../geometrodynamics/bulk/history_action.py): Pin/SU(2)
  return data and the already-distinguished rules for composing based loops;
- [transaction/network.py](../geometrodynamics/transaction/network.py) and
  [transaction/derived_network.py](../geometrodynamics/transaction/derived_network.py):
  any existing physical transport, gluing or response that can be related
  explicitly to these two triangle histories.

For each candidate, record its actual variables, source equation, domain,
boundary conditions and map to `(x1,x2)`. State whether it applies to two
disconnected preparations or only after adding a connection/intervention.
Independently prepared initial data do not forbid a later interaction,
but such an interaction changes the joint problem and must be identified.

Do not multiply matrices based in different source frames without deriving
the required transport identification. The existence of a formal cross
contraction is not evidence that it enters the physical weight. Likewise,
sequential concatenation of loops is not automatically parallel composition
of two independent systems.

Any claim that a new invariant is necessary must cite a concrete inherited
constraint or response depending on it. If no such map or rule exists,
report the repository gap; do not fill it with a chosen joint potential.
Position-dependent reshaping of the two phase windows can also change the
joint density while preserving the zero set. Such a construction must be
ledgered as a new conditioning prescription, not as geometry forcing a
joint invariant.

### Q3. Test reduction along level sets, not by fitting correlations

For every physically licensed rule found in Q2, derive its dependence
analytically where possible. A positive factorization result requires an
identity on the regular domain, including branch data and the correct
reference measure. A grid or regression fit alone cannot prove sufficiency.

For a negative result, construct two regular configurations with the same
candidate statistic but different required density/constraint values at
fixed settings and sectors. Prove equality of the statistics and inequality
of the relevant values analytically, or bound both errors sufficiently to
certify the separation. A numerical failure to find a pair proves nothing.

Use the exact coordinate `D_i=t_i+sqrt(2t_i) cos(psi_i)`. Pair-statistic
level sets have the independent reflection branches `psi_i -> -psi_i`.
Product-statistic level sets can be explored with
`D2=p0/D1` where both values lie strictly inside their allowed ranges.
These constructions must not silently cross punctures or vary the apparatus.
At `t1=t2=1`, both `(D1,D2)=(1,2)` and `(sqrt(2),sqrt(2))` are regular,
same-product configurations; the known cubic product is a negative control
for claiming universal product sufficiency. It is not a new physical choice.

Only after the statistic and the physical scope are established may P3 be
invoked. A density that happens to depend on `D1 D2` does not prove its four
hypotheses, particularly universal reuse of `Phi` on a composite scalar.

## 5. Frozen numerical controls for the future implementation

These settings are fixed before running any round-10 numerical experiment.
They check analytic statements; none is a numerical Born-rule search.

- Use dimensionless unit spheres and all 16 sectors. Seed `20260907` for
  reproducible auxiliary samples. Canonical pair 1 is `a1=e_z`,
  `b1=(sin(gamma1),0,cos(gamma1))`. Pair 2 has the same form rotated by
  `R=I` or `R=R_x(0.61) R_z(0.37)` (active right-handed rotations).
  Test angle pairs `(0.35,0.7)`, `(1.0,1.3)`, `(pi/2,pi/2)`, `(2.4,2.7)`.
  For the three-factor associativity control, use a separately labelled
  independent copy of pair 1 as the third factor.
- Analytic and independently computed normal Gram matrices, metric pullbacks,
  common-frame covariance, copy exchange and sector conventions must agree
  to relative `1e-10` on regular points with `|D_i|/|q_i|>=0.05`.
  Central differences use steps `1e-4,5e-5,2.5e-5`; require final relative
  error below `1e-6` and improvement unless already below `1e-10`.
- Compute reference circle integrals by independent quadrature and by
  uniform arclength grids of `512,1024,2048` points per factor. Split
  quadrature at known punctures. Require normalized sector probabilities
  to agree within `1e-6` at the final grid, and product marginals within
  `1e-10` when evaluated from the independently converged sector masses.
- For finite windows use sphere coordinates
  `x=sqrt(1-z^2) r(psi)+z qhat`, with area element `dpsi dz`.
  Evaluate the window inequalities directly, using adaptive quadrature or
  root-resolved integration; do not insert the limiting coarea density as
  the integrand. Set `(epsilon1,epsilon2)=(e,e),(e,2e),(2e,e)` for
  `e=0.04,0.02,0.01,0.005`. Target absolute integration error `1e-7` in
  normalized probabilities and final-window discrepancy below `2e-3`.
  Demonstrate convergence or justify a bound analytically; if unresolved,
  report it rather than changing the widths or acceptance band silently.
- Excise `|D_i|/|q_i|<eta` at `eta=0.02,0.01,0.005` for regular coarea
  comparisons and supply a vanishing-mass bound, including intersections
  of the two excised sets. Finite-window comparisons must address the
  excluded neighborhoods, not merely discard them from the target measure.
- A proposed level-set counterexample must match its statistic within
  `1e-12` in absolute error, have independently bounded evaluation errors
  below `1e-10`, and separate normalized or comparably scaled values by
  at least `1e-6`. An exact symbolic certificate takes precedence over
  floating-point thresholds. Record the normalization used before testing.

Do not introduce a fitted `Phi`, fit a power exponent, optimize CHSH, or
scan a coupling to target `2sqrt(2)`. The prior no-power-law theorem and
round-7/9 quadratic identity may be checked as historical regression oracles
only. Ordinary coordinate and normalization identities must be labelled as
such rather than counted as independent physical tests.

## 6. Verdicts and gates

Report three separate fields, not one label that conflates geometry and
weight selection:

1. **Reference composition:** `INDEPENDENT_PHASE_PRODUCT_VERIFIED`, or
   `UNRESOLVED`. This confirms the inherited conditional measure only.
2. **Reduction of each specified joint rule:** `PRODUCT_STATISTIC_SUFFICIENT`,
   `PAIR_STATISTIC_SUFFICIENT_PRODUCT_INSUFFICIENT`,
   `ADDITIONAL_JOINT_INVARIANT_REQUIRED`, or `UNRESOLVED`. Give the rule's
   provenance and whether it belongs to the independent or connected problem.
   Product sufficiency requires a factorization proof; pair-only requires
   that proof plus a product-level counterexample; additional data requires
   a certified pair-level counterexample for a physically required quantity.
3. **Consequence for selection:** `NO_SELECTION_FROM_INDEPENDENCE`,
   `CONDITIONAL_SCALAR_PRODUCT_CLASS_OBSTRUCTED`,
   `ROUND9_SCALAR_REDUCTION_NOT_CLOSED_UNDER_SPECIFIED_COMPOSITION`, or
   `JOINT_WEIGHT_RULE_UNSPECIFIED`. Invoke the conditional obstruction only
   with an explicit ledger for all P3 hypotheses; distinguish any merely
   assumed hypotheses from ones established for BAM.

The expected reference result is already stated in section 2. No outcome
for the existence or sufficiency of an inherited connected rule is predicted.
A missing rule is an informative repository gap, not a universal no-go.
Finding an extra invariant also does not automatically exclude all cubic
or power-law factors inside a richer model; it invalidates their claimed
completeness for that specified joint rule.

Failed mandatory verification yields `UNRESOLVED` for the affected claim
and a nonzero probe exit. Separate `checks_passed` from physical verdicts.
The existence of a passing baseline must not hide failure of a claimed
factorization, and failure of one candidate must not certify another.

## 7. Deliverables and limits

This request delivers the protocol only. A later implementation should add
one reusable joint-closure module, independent geometric and level-set
controls, an archived report with the input ledger and raw errors, and a
write-up separating prior results from new findings. Pin the accepted
pre-registration commit in those files. Preserve earlier freezes, archives,
field solvers and their historical numerical results.

This round neither derives a Born rule nor constructs an operational
source-local readout. It does not impose a Hilbert tensor product, identify
the TT director with triangle histories, or resolve the causality gate.
Its contribution is to establish which joint data the existing classical
rules actually require, and therefore which later selection arguments have
a justified premise.
